A few things to know

Sign in at slang.run and keep the recovery code shown at signup. Your saved blueprints follow your account across browsers. Email recovery is not enabled.

Public deployments run at <deployment-id>.slangapps.com. Anyone with their URL can invoke them. Capacity is limited to five deployments per account; stop or remove programs you no longer need.
