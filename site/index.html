A few things to know

A browser cookie keeps your workspace separate from other visitors. Saved programs stay for 30 days of inactivity. Export a backup before clearing cookies or changing browsers.

Running programs stop after 20 minutes of inactivity. If the playground is full, try again in a few minutes.
